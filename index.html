About PixelGallery

PixelGallery is a responsive image gallery built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Users can browse photos of cars, animals and cities, search by name or category, and open any image in a large viewer with next and previous buttons. They can also save favorites, upload their own images and apply image filters. The layout works smoothly on mobile, tablet and desktop screens.
